How It Works

1. Copy Freely

Hit your copy shortcut (or Cmd+C) on any item—text, image, file. It queues instantly. Repeat for everything you need.

2. Review and Reorder

Open Paste Queue from the menubar. Drag to reorder. Remove what you don’t need.

3. Paste on Demand

Switch to your destination. Use the paste shortcut for next item, click for out-of-order, or batch paste all.

4. Clear and Repeat

Queue empties automatically after pasting all, or clear manually. Ready for the next task.
